## Distinguishing the counters

Canon 24 contains two different counts.

$$
N_{\mathrm{active\ rows}}=54,
\qquad
N_{\mathrm{ledger\ counter}}=50.
$$

`54` is the number of rows in the current active claim table. `50` is the cumulative counter in the change
history of a particular carrier/gauge programme. They differ because several early empirical atoms were not
included in that change-history counter.

## E0–E5 — Six empirical atoms

### E0 `ULR-CONV-EXIST`

For a limited set of concept/model pairs, relational geometry exceeds a shuffled null. Shared alignment is
supported for those pairs; an observer-independent common space is not.

### E1 `ULR-CONV-INDEP`

Independent vision-only × language-only pairs also exceed chance. Joint multimodal training therefore cannot
explain the entire phenomenon, but this does not imply that every modality converges into one space.

### E2 `ULR-CONV-DIM`

20 of 25 CIFAR dimensions and 24 of 25 Caltech dimensions exceeded the held-out null. The result supports a
multidimensional phenomenon, but it does not identify 17, 20, or 24 as an exact intrinsic dimension.

### E3 `ULR-CONV-SEM`

Alignment has a graded association with the selected semantic/language proxies. Proxy correlation must not be
promoted to identity with meaning.

### E4 `ULR-CONV-ORIGIN`

The statistic strengthens during training from a near-chance initialisation, with a typed trend of
$\rho=0.881$. This is evidence of a learning trend, not of an exact formation time.

### E5 `ULR-EMP-GAUGE-ROBUST`

The T1/T2 exact results and T3 trend are preserved under the specified permutation × positive-diagonal typing.
This excludes an explanation based solely on raw-coordinate artefacts, but does not establish robustness under
general GL, nonlinear, or full-model gauges.

## O0–O2 — Three observer-relative role/port claims

### O0 `ULR-ROLE-PORT-SEPARATION`

A persistent writer and a cache writer with the same passive trajectory and intact output separate under the
declared reset/clone response $HRb$. The response is preserved under GL co-transformation.

The permitted conclusion is that a role unidentifiable through passive observation can become identifiable
through a richer physical interface. This is not a universal role ontology for real Transformers.

### O1 `ULR-PERSISTENT-SUBSPACE-IDENT-BOUNDARY`

For reset response $Y_R=HRB$, a full-column-rank read port $H$ and a full-row-rank preparation port $B$
recover $R$, whereas partial ports determine only $HRB$. Even matrices $R$ of different rank can produce
the same partial response.

The identified object is not $R$ itself, but $[R]_{H,B}$.

### O2 `ULR-OBSERVER-ROLE-DISCRIMINATION`

Once a role $C\in\{L,I\}$, observer filtration $\mathcal F^O$, and transcript laws $P_L,P_I$ are declared,
exact deterministic discrimination is possible if and only if $C$ is $\mathcal F^O$-measurable. For equal
priors, Bayes error is characterised by total variation:

$$
R^*=\frac{1-\lVert P_L-P_I\rVert_{\mathrm{TV}}}{2}.
$$

Adaptive distinguishability over admissible policies $\Pi$ is characterised by
$\sup_{\pi\in\Pi}\lVert P_L^\pi-P_I^\pi\rVert_{\mathrm{TV}}$. Under a fixed infinite policy, finite-prefix
error tends to zero if and only if the induced full path laws are mutually singular.

## N0 — One small-neural application

### `ULR-NEURAL-ROLE-PORT-ABSORPTION`

In a 32-seed engineered family, the same learned neural core is connected either to recurrent hidden state or to
an attention cache. Passive outputs and activations are pairwise exact, while reset/clone responses separate for
every seed. The typed response $Q$ predicts held-out partial-clone transfer better than the passive predictive
baseline.

However, raw port-response B3 and declared-routing B4 make nearly identical predictions, so the preregistered
nonabsorption gate is not passed. The neural realisation of the exact toy remains an active result, but it does
not establish a neural-specific object, learned organisation, a large-model law, or a ULR ontology.
