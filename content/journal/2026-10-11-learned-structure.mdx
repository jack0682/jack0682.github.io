## The question now

What has formed through training that lets a model interpret relations and
respond to new situations? How does a momentary latent activation express
that structure, and what could observations of those activations establish?

The October research review separates three obligations:

1. **Formation:** explain what training selects across experiences.
2. **Realization:** show how the selected structure constrains processing and
   responses within a declared family of situations.
3. **Identification:** determine what observations can recover, and which
   differences between possible models remain invisible.

These are connected questions, but a result about one does not settle the
others. An object that predicts responses need not explain how training formed
it. Recovering a generative factor need not recover the network's mechanism.

## A more precise research agenda

I am comparing candidate descriptions through sparse dictionaries, predictive
states, algebraic realizations, representation symmetries, causal abstraction,
and learning dynamics. The aim is to specify what each description preserves
and what evidence could distinguish it from its rivals.

The observation contract matters: unpaired activations, input-activation pairs,
trajectories, task responses, interventions, and training records are different
sources of evidence. The next step is to choose a target and an observation
regime before claiming that the target can be recovered.

Related mathematical work asks how exact computational certificates can
support identifiability claims. Local rank calculations, generic statements,
and uniqueness for a particular observed instance remain separate obligations.
The current work does not establish a general global-uniqueness theorem or a
new computational-complexity result.

## Status and limits

This is a **working research direction and literature synthesis**, not a new
neural result or a canonical promotion. It does not establish that passive
latent observations determine arbitrary training-formed mechanisms. It also
does not prove that all local observations are insufficient: the meaning of
"local" and the available assumptions must be specified first.

The [current ULR status](/ulr/current-status/) remains **Canon 24 · Ledger 105 ·
Progress 31**, with **M8 = NO** for the declared candidate registry. The
[founding motivation](/ulr/motivation/) and
[reopening conditions](/ulr/open-problems/) remain the reference points for
any stronger claim.
