## What the project is for

[RobotTransformation](https://github.com/jack0682/RobotTransformation) explores
a common way to describe tasks, authority, results, and recovery across robots,
equipment, and services. The practical aim is to reduce the work a system
integrator spends authoring workflows, diagnosing failures, recovering from
uncertain outcomes, and handing a system over.

This is a **personal project, separate from employer work**. Its public
development home brings together the earlier RX Platform and RX Solutions
sources while preserving their histories.

## What the preview covers

The [v0.1.0 release](https://github.com/jack0682/RobotTransformation/releases/tag/v0.1.0),
published on October 8, records a simulation workflow for configuring a
material alignment/regrasp task, saving and reopening its configuration,
executing that version, and inspecting its result on the action graph.

The workflow distinguishes completion, confirmed failure, an unknown outcome
with retained resource custody, and reconciliation after a lost start response.
These distinctions matter because losing a response is not evidence that the
underlying work never happened.

## Verification status as of October 11

The release notes record **post-publication verification failures** following
earlier passing promotion checks. Guarded-shutdown confirmation and
lost-start-response effect evidence remain under investigation. The original
changed-material failure also remains part of the unresolved record.

This is a source preview, **not an installable product or qualification for
physical operation**. A release tag, a passing simulation, and a physical
completion or safety claim are different kinds of evidence.
