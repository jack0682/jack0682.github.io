*Rewritten 2026-07-10 against `onn_ws/ONN`. The earlier draft stated stability
"in cohomological terms" and cited numbers (`99.75%`, $\mathcal{L}_{\text{topo}}
= 0.0234$) that are **not in the source**; both are removed. The acronym is the
published paper's **Ontological Real-Time Semantic Fabric**.*

## What ORTSF is

**ORTSF** was designed to turn an ONN reasoning trace into delay-aware control
commands. The current source supports two narrower, model-specific artifacts:
a phase-margin gate in a guarded pipeline and a single-mode additive scalar
condition. Neither is a cohomological or deployed-system certificate.

## Operator family

| Operator class | Symbol / mechanism | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Projection** | $T_{\text{proj}}$ | Projects the ONN latent state onto the typed fibre of the target ontology. |
| **Consensus** | $T_{\text{cons}}$ | Aggregates neighbouring fibres toward local agreement. |
| **Composite** | $T = T_{\text{proj}} \circ T_{\text{cons}}$ | The core update; a spine contraction $\rho_{\mathrm{ONN}} = \rho_{\mathrm{drift}} < 1$. |
| **Guarded pipeline** | `GuardedORTSFPipeline` | Computes the delay margin from the plant spectrum and admits commands only while $\text{delay} < \Delta t_{\max}$; else falls back to a zero-velocity command. |
| **Drift guard** | KL-divergence monitor (A3$'$) | Detects regime shifts that would invalidate the stationarity assumption. |

## The scoped control results

**Model-level phase-margin gate:** for the constructed linear plant and the
integrating-controller model $C(s)=K/s$,

$$
\Delta t_{\max} = \frac{\varphi_{\mathrm{PM}}}{\omega_c}, \qquad
\varphi_{\mathrm{PM}} = \frac{\pi}{2} - \arctan\!\left(\frac{\omega_c}{p}\right),
\quad p = \lambda_2 + \mu.
$$

This threshold is implemented and tested in `GuardedORTSFPipeline`, which is not
the default deployed control path.

**Single-mode additive recurrence:** for
$e_{t+1}=\rho e_t-K_c e_{t-d}$, the sufficient condition
$\rho+|K_c|<1$ gives Schur stability for every integer delay $d\geq1$. It does
not cover coupled modes, and $K_c$ is not mapped to the shipped multi-joint PD
controller. Full statement and scope are on the
[delay analysis page](/onn/theorem-delay-robust-bound/).

## Honest scope

- **Systems completion, not novelty** — both are standard control arguments for
  their stated models, not a general nonlinear closed-loop theorem.
- **No coupled multimode or deployed-system certificate.** The actuation-operator
  bound, physical gain mapping, and deployed-path validation remain open.
- **No cohomological stability bounds.** The claim that cohomological invariants
  double as Lyapunov certificates is [withdrawn](/onn/theorem-cohomological-stability/).
- **No `τ_max = 177 μs`, no `99.75%`.** These are not in the source and are removed.
- **Ontology-morphism $\to$ feedback-gain functor:** proposed, **not established**;
  left as an open direction.

## Open directions

The coupled multimode bound through the actuation operator, mapping the abstract
$K_c$ to the physical PD/interpolation law, and validating the guarded path in
deployment.
