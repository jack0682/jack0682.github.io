## RobotTransformation — personal simulation source preview

The engineering question is practical: how can one system integrator author,
diagnose, recover, and hand over workflows spanning different robots and
pieces of equipment?

[RobotTransformation](https://github.com/jack0682/RobotTransformation) is a
personal project, separate from employer work, exploring common task,
authority, state, result, and recovery contracts. Development has moved here
from [RX Platform](https://github.com/jack0682/rx-platform) and
[RX Solutions](https://github.com/jack0682/rx-solutions); their source histories
remain available.

The [October 8 v0.1.0 source preview](https://github.com/jack0682/RobotTransformation/releases/tag/v0.1.0)
covers configuring, saving, reopening, running, and inspecting a material
alignment/regrasp workflow in simulation. Post-publication verification
failures remain unresolved. It is not yet qualified for physical deployment
or available as an installable product.

Read the [preview and verification update](/journal/2026-10-08-robottransformation-preview/).

## AntBot navigation — merged open-source contribution

[AntBot PR #1](https://github.com/ROBOTIS-move/antbot/pull/1), merged on April 13,
2026, separates real-robot and simulation launch paths, normalizes LiDAR scans
for the real platform, updates Nav2 controller and costmap configuration, and
adds operating documentation. The contribution makes different sensing and
controller assumptions explicit in the two environments.

## Constrained assembly — Soma Cube

The [Soma Cube preprint](/papers/soma-cube-assembly-dqn/) studies legal-action
masked DQN and robot regrasp constraints on a Doosan M0609. Jaehong Oh,
Seungjun Jung, and Sawoong Kim are the coauthors; I am the first author of the
August 2025 preprint.

See the [project repository](https://github.com/jack0682/Somacube_RL_project)
and [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21272) for the reported methods
and results. These experimental results are not a general production or
functional-safety qualification.
